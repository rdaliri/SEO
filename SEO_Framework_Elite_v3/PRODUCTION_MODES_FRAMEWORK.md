# Production Modes Framework

## Purpose
Allow scalable execution based on business goals and publishing velocity.

---

# Available Modes

## Strict Mode
Use for:
- pillar pages
- high-value landing pages
- authority content

Includes:
- full audits
- deep entity mapping
- extensive QA

---

## Fast Production Mode
Use for:
- standard blog posts
- supporting content

Includes:
- reduced QA
- faster generation
- simplified structure

---

## Bulk Production Mode
Use for:
- scalable content systems
- large publishing operations

Includes:
- standardized templates
- modular sections
- batch workflows

---

## Local SEO Mode
Use for:
- service-area pages
- city pages
- local landing pages

Includes:
- geographic validation
- local entity checks
- duplicate prevention

---

## Conversion-Focused Mode
Use for:
- lead generation pages
- sales pages
- commercial intent pages

Includes:
- CTA optimization
- trust signal placement
- conversion structure review

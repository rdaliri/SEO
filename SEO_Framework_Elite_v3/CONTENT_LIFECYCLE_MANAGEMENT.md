# Content Lifecycle Management

## Purpose
Maintain long-term SEO performance.

---

# Content Refresh Rules

Review pages regularly for:
- outdated statistics
- broken links
- SERP changes
- competitor improvements
- outdated screenshots
- declining rankings

---

# Decay Detection

Potential decay indicators:
- traffic decline
- CTR decline
- reduced impressions
- outdated metadata
- stale references

---

# Refresh Priority Levels

## High Priority
- Commercial pages
- Local landing pages
- High-converting pages

## Medium Priority
- Informational articles
- Supporting blog posts

## Low Priority
- Archived references
- Legacy updates

---

# Update Actions

Possible actions:
- refresh sections
- improve semantic coverage
- add FAQs
- improve media
- improve internal linking
- consolidate duplicate pages
- prune weak pages

---

# Content Consolidation

Merge pages when:
- keyword overlap exists
- intent overlap exists
- traffic cannibalization occurs

# Module 04: Local and Scaled Content

## Role
Local SEO and Scaled Content Risk Specialist.

## Objective
Approve only location or scaled pages that have distinct user value, genuine evidence, and defensible indexation purpose.

## Local Page Rules
1. Prohibit fake offices, false service areas, invented local experience, duplicated reviews, and city-name substitution.
2. Require meaningful local differentiation such as staff, address/service-area facts, projects, regulations, logistics, pricing factors, availability, photos, reviews, or community context.
3. Recommend a standalone location page only when the location has distinct demand, operational relevance, and sufficient unique content.
4. When evidence is insufficient, recommend consolidation into a regional/service-area page or defer production.
5. Structured data must match visible, verified business facts.

## Scaled Content Rules
1. Every page requires a unique page role, intent, source inputs, value proposition, and evidence set.
2. Prohibit template-only substitution of city, service, product, demographic, or keyword variables.
3. Run duplicate/near-duplicate checks before approval.
4. Apply critical checks to every item and deep QA to a risk-based sample of at least 10% or 10 items, whichever is greater, capped only by explicit project constraints.
5. Fail the batch when repeated unsupported claims, doorway patterns, systematic duplication, or incorrect page roles are found.
6. Correct the template/root cause before regenerating affected items.

## Output
- Page viability table
- Unique-evidence requirements per page
- Doorway/scaled-risk assessment
- Architecture and internal links
- Batch QA plan and sample findings
- Approve, consolidate, defer, or reject decision

## v7 Specialist Guidance
- Require genuine geographic purpose, distinct customer concerns, local operational details, unique proof, and a differentiated conversion path.
- Reject city-name substitution, repeated heading patterns, cloned FAQs, and unsupported local-presence claims.
- Validate NAP and local schema only from supplied or observed evidence.
- Original local imagery is preferred; generated local concepts must not imply a real storefront, staff member, landmark relationship, or service presence without evidence.


## v7.4 Local, Scaled, Doorway, and Reputation-Abuse Controls

For every local or scaled set:

- prove a distinct user task and page role for each indexable page;
- reject city/service substitutions without genuine local or service-specific value;
- audit doorway behavior and common-destination funneling;
- audit scaled content abuse regardless of whether humans, AI, automation, or mixed methods produced it;
- audit third-party or white-label sections for site reputation abuse;
- require genuine local proof and service-scope confirmation;
- block fake offices, service areas, staff, reviews, prices, events, and local references;
- document index/noindex, canonical, consolidation, or removal decisions for weak pages.

Use Module 09 for similarity evidence and Module 10 for policy interpretation before Module 07.

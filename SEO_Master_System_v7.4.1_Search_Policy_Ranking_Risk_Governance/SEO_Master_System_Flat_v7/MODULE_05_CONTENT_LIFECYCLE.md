# Module 05: Content Lifecycle

## Role
Content Lifecycle and Consolidation Analyst.

## Objective
Choose retain, refresh, merge, redirect, noindex, or retire using dated evidence and page-role logic.

## Operational Rules
1. Review URL, age, update history, intent fit, content quality, overlap, indexation, backlinks, performance, and conversions when available.
2. Do not treat traffic decline alone as proof of decay.
3. When analytics or ranking evidence is unavailable, perform a content/architecture assessment and mark performance conclusions `NOT TESTED`.
4. Preserve unique value, relevant links, and conversion assets during consolidation.
5. Specify redirect/canonical/noindex actions only when technically appropriate and explain the destination or retained role.
6. Define post-change measurement and rollback triggers.

## Output
- Evidence table
- Decay, overlap, and intent diagnosis
- URL action matrix
- Refresh/consolidation brief
- Redirect/internal-link changes
- Measurement and retest plan

## v7 Specialist Guidance
- Detect decay using supplied or observed performance, freshness, accuracy, overlap, and business-priority evidence.
- Choose retain, refresh, consolidate, redirect, noindex, or retire based on page role and implementation risk.
- Preserve approved context and record changed decisions in the project context or task register.


## v7.4 Lifecycle, Migration, and Recovery Risk Controls

Before refresh, merge, redirect, retire, migrate, or repurpose decisions:

- check expired-domain abuse and topical-purpose mismatch;
- reject deceptive redirects and policy circumvention;
- map every changed URL to a legitimate equivalent destination or appropriate removal status;
- preserve or intentionally replace valuable internal links and canonical signals;
- update sitemaps, structured data, metadata, and indexation directives consistently;
- do not mass-delete or change dates solely to appear fresh or influence rankings;
- distinguish manual actions, security issues, technical regressions, algorithmic reassessment, and demand changes;
- define monitoring, owners, rollback conditions, and retests.

Route policy and ranking-risk conclusions through Module 10.

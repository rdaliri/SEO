# Reference Playbook: Content Structures v7

## Status
Reference-only. Select structures by intent and page role; do not use them as rigid formulas.

## Commercial or Service Page
Direct value statement; audience/problem; solution; process; proof; decision criteria; limitations; FAQs where useful; CTA.

## Informational Article
Direct answer; definitions/context; key sub-questions; examples or evidence; limitations; related actions and internal links.

## Local Page
Unique local purpose; service relevance; operating context; local proof; service area details; distinct FAQs; conversion path.

## Technical Audit Summary
Scope; observed findings; evidence state; severity; impact; recommendation; owner; acceptance test.

## Comparison Page
Decision context; comparison criteria; evidence-backed table; tradeoffs; fit by use case; transparent limitations.

Structure must follow evidence and user need. No fixed word counts or mandatory section quotas.
